Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

OpenChat is built with privacy and user control at its core. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what stays under your control.

1. Data You Control

OpenChat is a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) client. You connect your own API keys from AI providers you choose. Those keys are stored securely in your device’s Keychain and are only used to send requests directly to the providers you select. We never receive, store, or process your API keys or chat messages on our servers.

2. Conversations Stay on Your Device

Your chat history is stored locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData framework. It is not synced to our servers. Temporary chats are not persisted at all.

3. Optional Photos and Camera

OpenChat can access photos you select or images you capture through the camera. These are used locally to let the AI analyze images you choose to share. The app does not upload your photos or camera data to our infrastructure.

4. Optional Microphone

The microphone is used only when you enable voice input for transcription. Audio is processed locally or by the speech provider you choose, not by us.

5. No Third-Party Analytics or Ads

OpenChat does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking. We do not sell your data.

6. Open Source

OpenChat is open source. You can inspect the code, build it yourself, and verify how data is handled.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at the issue tracker or email listed in the App Store listing.
